# Mission Reflection

Completing Mission 3 helped me understand that choosing a cloud platform depends on the needs of an organization. AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud provide many similar services, including virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, identity management, and Kubernetes. However, each provider has different strengths that can make it more suitable for a particular business requirement.

The cloud platform that impressed me the most was AWS because of its wide range of services and global infrastructure. I learned that AWS can support different types of workloads, from small startup applications to large enterprise and e-commerce systems. Azure also stood out because of its strong integration with Microsoft technologies, while Google Cloud impressed me with its AI, machine learning, and Kubernetes capabilities.

Business requirements have an important role in cloud platform selection. Factors such as budget, existing technology, scalability, performance, security, location, and application requirements should be considered before choosing a provider. For example, a university that already uses Microsoft technologies may benefit more from Azure, while an AI research company may find Google Cloud more suitable for its workloads.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me to look at technology from both a technical and business perspective. I learned that a cloud engineer should not simply choose the most popular provider. Instead, the provider should be selected based on how well its services solve the client's specific problems.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved because I added organized research, comparison tables, client recommendations, a decision matrix, screenshots, and technical documentation. This mission also helped me improve my understanding of the differences between AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud while practicing Markdown and cloud-service evaluation.
